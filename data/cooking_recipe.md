# Classic Italian Pasta Carbonara Recipe

Pasta Carbonara is a classic Roman dish. To prepare it, boil spaghetti in salted water. In a separate pan, crisp some guanciale or pancetta. Whisk fresh egg yolks with grated Pecorino Romano cheese and plenty of freshly cracked black pepper. Combine the hot drained pasta with the meat, remove from heat, and quickly stir in the egg mixture to create a creamy sauce.
