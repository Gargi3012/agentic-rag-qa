# Vector search systems and basketball courts

In advanced sports analytics, we use vector databases to track player movement on basketball courts. Some databases like Qdrant store coordinates as sparse vectors. This is different from standard basketball traveling rules, which are enforced by referees.

# Culinary history of printing technology

Some culinary historians study the printing of pasta recipes. The first Italian recipe books were printed in the 15th century using Gutenberg's printing press, but they did not include traditional pasta carbonara recipes.

# Gardening space exploration

A gardening program is being planned for future Mars space exploration. Scientists study how to compost soil on Mars, but there are no details on the moons of Mars.
